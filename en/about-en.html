About the Company

We are a company specialized in landscaping, selecting plants suitable for Makkah’s climate, and implementing high‑quality automatic irrigation systems. We operate in Al‑Jumum and Makkah, focusing on practical solutions and long‑lasting quality in every project we deliver.

Our Vision

To be the first choice for landscaping and irrigation systems in the Makkah region, by delivering high‑quality work that reflects professionalism and trust.

Our Mission

Providing practical green solutions suitable for the Kingdom’s environment, with a focus on quality, fast execution, and selecting plants suitable for hot climates.

Our Values

  • Quality in execution
  • Selecting heat‑resistant plants
  • Commitment to deadlines
  • Practical solutions that last
  • Clear and respectful customer service

Our Experience

We have hands‑on experience in designing and implementing irrigation networks, selecting suitable plants, and preparing gardens and outdoor spaces tailored to the nature of Makkah and Al‑Jumum. We focus on the small details that make a big difference in the final result.
