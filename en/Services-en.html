Our Services at Integrated Landscape Solutions

At Integrated Landscape Solutions, we believe that designing outdoor spaces is not just about arranging plants — it is a complete engineering process that combines beauty, sustainability, and operational efficiency. We provide specialized services that ensure a balanced, functional outdoor environment designed to last for many years without issues.

Outdoor Site & Landscape Design

Every project begins with studying the site, soil type, sun direction, and usage patterns. We create a fully engineered plan that includes suitable plant distribution, walking paths, seating areas, outdoor lighting, water features, and decorative elements — all aimed at creating a comfortable and beautiful space that fits your lifestyle.

Smart Irrigation Network Installation

Irrigation systems are the backbone of any successful landscape project. We provide drip and sprinkler systems, fully automated setups, and moisture sensors to save water — ensuring a highly efficient network with reduced consumption and easy maintenance.

Supplying & Installing Climate‑Suitable Plants

We carefully select plants based on regional temperature, soil type, required maintenance level, and overall design style. We provide strong, climate‑resistant plants that thrive for long periods without issues.

Garden & Outdoor Space Maintenance

We offer monthly or seasonal maintenance programs including lawn trimming, pathway cleaning, irrigation system inspection, plant treatment, and space rearrangement when needed — ensuring long‑lasting beauty and functionality.

Complete Outdoor Décor Solutions

We provide integrated outdoor décor solutions such as natural stones, ground lighting, fountains, waterfalls, outdoor seating, and shading systems — each element blending seamlessly with the overall design and adding value to the space.

Our Mission

We don’t just provide a service — we deliver a complete solution that makes your outdoor space more beautiful, more comfortable, and more sustainable.
